SCOUT LAW

  1. A scout's Honour is to be trusted.
  2. A scout is a good citize who respects GOD, own country,
    parents, employees, scouters and others.
  3. A scout's duty is to be useful and to help others.
  4. A scout is a friend to all and a kin to every other scouter,
    no matter what the coutry, community, religion the other may belong.
  5. A scout is kind and has respect for others.
  6. A scout is kind to animals, learn about nature, and is
    concerned of it's protection.
  7. A scout obeys lawful orders from those in authority.
  8. a scout is cheerful and brave under all difficulties.
  9. a scout makes good use of all things.
  10. a scout is clean in appearance, thoughts, words and deeds.
