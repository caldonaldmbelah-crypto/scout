PATROL SYSTEM

Is a method, which the Scout training & activities rest on. Is a unique and dynamic method by which a Scout unit operates through small groups/patrols.
Operations of the Patrol Systems
• The Scout Leader trains the patrol leaders thoroughly well
• The patrol leaders, in turn using the knowledge and skills gained to train their scouts in their patrols during patrol meetings
• Each patrol guarantees their members safety, security and opportunities to explore, discover and want to know.
Importance of Patrol System
o Gives an opportunity to lead and to be led by the peers
o Opportunity to develop socially and learn how to relate and work with others at the unit and in their families
o Makes it easy to run unit activities and gives the Scouts opportunity to participate in running unit activities
o To learn how to think and make plans for others {decision making}
o To learn to take responsibilities
o To learn how to organize effectively
o To gain leadership qualities
o Provides security to members/ guarantees safety to members
o Overcomes inhibition in members
o Helps cultivate and build team spirit and fraternity among Scouts.
Characteristics of a Patrol system
o A patrol is a group of 6-8 scouts
o Each Patrol must have a Patrol badge, flag, APL and name
o Each Patrol must have Patrol records kept by the Patrol Scribe
o Team spirit and brotherhood must be cultivated in the Patrol
o Each member of the Patrol should have a specific role to play e.g.
• Patrol Leader (Overall leader)
• Assistant Patrol Leader (Second in leader
ship) • Patrol Grub Master
• Patrol Den Keeper
• Patrol Librarian
• Patrol Cheer Leader
• Patrol Hike Leader

Functions/ roles
a.) Patrol Leader
Within a unit, a Patrol Leader performs the following tasks:
1. Commands the Patrol
2. Represent the Patrol in C.O.H
3. Locate duties to other members
4. Leads Patrols during Patrol activities
5. Trains members of the Patrol
6. Introduce new ideas to their Patrol members
7. Performs duties assigned by C.O.H and Scout Leader
8. Keeps Patrol records
9. Presents Scout for investiture

A good Patrol Leader is one who is:
• Brave and courageous
• Understanding,
• Friendly and sympathetic
• Knowledgeable and creative
• Responsible • Obedient to the Court of Honour and to the Scout Leader
• Firm, fair and flexible in decision-making

b.) Assistant patrol leader
• Assists the Patrol leader in day-to-day activities of the administration of the unit
• However, s/he may have specific duties as assigned by the Patrol leader.

c.)Patrol grub master
• Oversees the condition of the dry and wet pits
• Organizes food and rations
• Determines the menu for a camp hike
• Buys and stores the food
• Keeps records of food use
d.)Patrol den keeper
• In charge of Patrol Corner or den
• Selects any areas to be used by the Patrol
• Makes sure the area used by the Patrol for camping is left in better condition than how the Patrol found it

e) Patrol librarian
• Ensures the safety of Patrol books
• Keeps a record of the library including receipts of books, issuing of books, and dates of return, etc.
• Assists in gathering information materials for the Patrol
• Keeps a conducive atmosphere within the library, especially during the camp

f)Patrol cheer master
• Acts as Patrol cheerleader
• Acts as campfire leader
• Acts as spirit person

g) Hike master/camp master
• Plans and organizes hikes and camps
• Helps in map-reading
• Makes the maps and compasses for the Patrol Gear Master
• Maintains Patrol equipment, including camping gear

h)Scribe
• Keeps Patrol log book and albums
• Keeps records of Patrol in council
• Produces Patrol newsletter

i)Patrol record keeper
• Looks after attendance register and address book
• Keeps records of equipment for the Patrol
• Keeps all other records as instructed by Patrol leader

j) Patrol first aider
• Trained/expert in first aid
• Looks after the first aid equipment
• Keeps records of all first aid applied in the course of Scouting

k)Patrol treasurer
• Looks after funds
• Keeps the cash book, receipts
• Prepares the budget for proposed activities

i) Patrol public relations and information
• Responsible for Patrol communications, notices and reminders
• Keeps the Patrol 'information board' up-to-date
• Takes and posts photographs
• Acts as a reporter in conjunction with the Patrol Scribe
Note: These are just suggestions.
Actual duties may differ depending on the circumstances.
